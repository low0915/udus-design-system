# Slide data and claims

The approved slide images and MES PDF are design references. Their example numbers, economic scenarios and product claims are not automatically live business data or verified customer results. Label illustrative data visibly. Reuse user-supplied actual data when available and retain its source/date.

The stock chart uses demo quantities totalling500units; the received/fulfilled trend uses separate daily event counts, not a matched order cohort or a certified backlog. Do not turn them into ROI, accuracy, speed or customer-performance claims. Keep required approvals before execution and distinguish operational completion, accounting posting and payment.

The plugin's product/marketing knowledge remains the broader reference for capabilities and claim corrections. This local sync covers approved slide styling, artwork and module icons; it does not certify every claim in legacy examples or the source PDF.
